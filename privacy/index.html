GlowBeam Flashlight

Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026 · Published by Thanh Mich LTD

GlowBeam Flashlight ("the App") is developed and published by Thanh Mich LTD ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what data the App collects, why, and how it is used.

1. What the App does

GlowBeam Flashlight is an offline flashlight and utility tool. It turns your device's LED into a torch, lets you blink it at a custom rhythm, turns your screen into a colored light, provides SOS and Disco light modes, and can flash the light as an alert when you receive a call, text message, or notification. The App works fully offline — no internet connection is required to use any lighting feature.

2. Information we collect

2.1 Data stored only on your device. Your app settings — flash rhythm (on/off time), screen-light color and brightness, which alerts are enabled, theme, text size, and language — are stored locally in your device's app storage. This data is never transmitted to us or to any third party.

2.2 Data collected automatically via third-party services. The App uses the following third-party SDKs, which may collect device identifiers and diagnostic data as described in Section 4:

  • Google AdMob and its mediation partners (including AppLovin, Pangle, and Unity Ads) — used for advertising.
  • Google Firebase — Analytics, Crashlytics, and Remote Config, used to understand app usage, diagnose crashes, and manage app configuration.
  • Adjust — used for install attribution and analytics.
  • Facebook (Meta) SDK — used for advertising and analytics.

These SDKs are integrated into the App and initialize when the App starts, even in versions where no ad is currently displayed. The data types involved are described in Section 4.

2.3 Notification access (used only for the Flash Alert feature). If you enable the optional "Flash Alert" feature and grant Notification Access, the App is able to detect that a notification has arrived (for example, an incoming call, text message, or app notification) so it can flash the light.

The App only reads the package name of the app that posted the notification to decide whether to trigger a flash — it does not read, store, log, or transmit the content, sender, or subject of any notification, call, or message. This feature is off by default and only active if you explicitly grant the permission and enable it in Flash Alert settings.

2.4 Camera permission (used only to control the flash). The App requests the Camera permission solely to access your device's torch control API to turn the LED torch on and off. The App never captures, records, or stores any photo, video, or camera preview.

2.5 Phone state permission (used only for call alerts). If you enable "Flash when: Call" in Flash Alert settings, the App uses the Read Phone State permission to detect when a call is ringing, so it can flash the light. The App does not access your call log, phone number, or contacts.

6. Data retention and deletion

The App does not require an account or sign-in, so there is no user profile to delete on our servers. Locally stored settings are removed automatically when you uninstall the App, or you can clear them at any time via your device's Settings › Apps › GlowBeam Flashlight › Storage › Clear data. For deletion of any data held by the third-party services listed in Section 5 (such as an advertising identifier), please refer to that provider's own privacy controls, or contact us below and we will assist.

7. Children's privacy

The App is a general-audience utility tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

8. Your choices

  • You can deny or revoke the Camera, Read Phone State, Notification Access, or Notification permissions at any time in your device Settings; the corresponding alert feature will simply be unavailable.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's Ads settings (Settings › Google › Ads, or Settings › Privacy › Ads on Android).
