SOS, Disco & flash alerts

An emergency signal, a party strobe, and a light that tells you who's calling.

SOS Light flashes the recognisable three-short, three-long, three-short pattern. Disco Light throws fast random pulses. Each says on screen what it does, so you're not guessing.

Flash Alert blinks the torch when a call, message, or notification arrives — useful when your phone is face-down or across the room. You pick which alerts count, and you can tell it not to flash while you're already looking at the screen.

FAQ
Why does it ask for the camera?

Only to reach the torch control — that is the system API every flashlight app uses to switch the LED. GlowBeam never takes a photo, records video, or keeps a camera preview open.

What does notification access do?

It lets the app notice that a notification arrived so it can flash the light. It only checks which app sent it. It never reads, stores, or sends the content, the sender, or the message text anywhere. It's optional and off until you turn it on.

Does the light stay on if I leave the app?

Yes. A lightweight background service keeps the torch or strobe running, with a notification you can use to turn it off.

Does it need internet?

No. Every light mode works fully offline.

Is anything paid or locked?

No. There's no account, no subscription and no in-app purchase — every feature is available from the first launch.

What languages does it speak?

18: English, Arabic, Bangla, German, Spanish, French, Hindi, Indonesian, Italian, Japanese, Korean, Burmese, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Thai, Turkish, Vietnamese and Chinese. Picking one applies it immediately.

My phone doesn't have a flash. Can I still use it?

Yes — the screen light, colour picker and brightness control work on any device. The app tells you plainly if no LED is available instead of failing silently.
